The print

  • What’s on it?

    Every tide of 2027

    All 365 days of tides at Jacksonville Beach, drawn as one ring around the beach. It’s 18 x 24 in, printed on thick matte paper and shipped unframed.

  • How do I read it?

    One line, one day

    Each line runs from that day’s low tide to its high tide. A longer line means a bigger swing. January sits at the top, and the year runs clockwise. Solid discs are full moons. Dashed rings are new moons.

    The tides are NOAA’s predictions for station 8720291, the Jacksonville Beach pier. Wind and storms move real tides around, so enjoy it on the wall, not at the boat ramp.

  • Will it fit my frame?

    Any 18 x 24 frame

    It fits a standard 18 x 24 in frame, sold at most frame and craft stores. No trimming needed.

Buying

    Stripe handles your card, and I never see your card number. Apple Pay and Google Pay work. No account needed.

  • Can I send it as a gift?

    Yes

    Enter their address at checkout and it ships straight to them. For Christmas, order by December 10.

    Within 12 hours

    Within 12 hours of ordering, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. If it hasn’t gone to print, I’ll change or cancel it and refund you in full. After that, it’s already being made for you.

Shipping

  • When will it get here?

    Usually 1 to 2 weeks

    It’s printed for you within 5 business days, then spends 3 to 8 business days in the mail. You’ll get a tracking link by email when it ships.

  • Where do you ship?

    Anywhere in the US

    All 50 states, free. I don’t ship outside the US yet.

  • It came rolled. Now what?

    Unroll it and frame it

    It can go straight into a poster hanger or a frame with plexiglass, right out of the tube.

    For the flattest result, lay it on a hard, flat surface, not carpet, with a few books on top overnight. Then frame it.

If something’s wrong

    I’ll replace it, free

    Within 30 days of delivery, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art with a photo. Pick a new print or a full refund. No need to send anything back.

  • It never showed up.

    I’ll track it down

    Check your tracking link first. Still nothing 30 days after the expected date? Email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. I’ll send a new one or refund you.

  • Can I return it?

    Only if something’s wrong

    Each print is made just for you, so I can’t take returns for a change of mind. Anything damaged, misprinted or lost is on me. The full policy is in Shipping and returns.
