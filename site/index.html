The story

I grew up at Jacksonville Beach, where fishing, surfing and having fun all revolved around the tides.

This print tells the future story of 2027. The moon sets the pace. The seasons push the tides to their highs and lows.

How to read it

Full moon or new moon? Scoot your towel back.

Each line is one day, from low tide to high tide. January starts at the top and the year runs clockwise. A longer line means a bigger swing. Solid discs are full moons. Dashed rings are new moons.
