Privacy

What I collect, why, and who else sees it.

In short

  • I collect only what I need to make and ship your print.
  • I never see or store your card number. Stripe does.
  • I never sell or rent your information.
  • Ask me to see or delete it any time.

Who I am

This site is run by Aaron Tarver, an artist in Jacksonville, Florida. “I” means me.

What I collect

  • When you buy: your name, email, shipping address and phone number if you give one. Stripe collects these at checkout.
  • When you join the collector list: your email.
  • When you visit: basic visit data, like pages viewed, device type and the ad or link that brought you, through cookies.

How I use it

  • To make, ship and support your order.
  • To email you about new pieces, if you joined the list.
  • To learn which ads and links work.

Who else sees it

Only the services that run this shop, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Stripe takes your payment.
  • Printful prints and ships your order, so it gets your name and address.
  • Kit stores the collector list and sends its emails.
  • Meta measures my Facebook and Instagram ads through its pixel.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site.

No one else gets your information. I don’t sell or rent it.

Cookies

The Meta pixel uses cookies to measure ads. You can block cookies in your browser and the site still works. You can also limit ads in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

Emails

Every collector list email has an unsubscribe link. Order emails, like your receipt and tracking, still come.

How long I keep it

Order records stay as long as tax law requires. Your collector list email stays until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your information, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. I’ll take care of it within 30 days.

Kids

Kids are welcome to look at the art. Buying and joining the collector list are for adults. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think I have, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art and I’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

Questions?Email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. I answer every message myself, within 48 hours.
