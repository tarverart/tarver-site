Shipping and returns

How your print gets to you, and what happens if something goes wrong.

In short

  • Free shipping anywhere in the US.
  • Usually at your door in 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Damaged, misprinted or lost? I replace it or refund you.
  • No returns for a change of mind. Each print is made to order.

Shipping

Where
All 50 US states. I don’t ship outside the US yet.
Cost
Free. Prices are in US dollars. Sales tax, if any, shows at checkout.
How long
Your order goes to print within 1 business day, after the 12 hour window to make changes. It’s printed within 5 more. It arrives 3 to 8 business days after it ships.
How
Rolled in a tube, sent by USPS, UPS or FedEx. My print partner, Printful, prints and ships it.
Tracking
A tracking link comes by email when it ships.
Holidays
For Christmas, order by December 10.

Please check your address at checkout. If a print comes back because of a wrong address, I can send it again for the cost of shipping.

Changes and cancellations

Within 12 hours of ordering, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. If it hasn’t gone to print, I’ll change or cancel it and refund you in full. Once printing starts, it can’t be canceled.

Damaged, misprinted or lost

Within 30 days of delivery, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art with a photo. You choose a new print or a full refund. Nothing to send back.

If it hasn’t arrived 30 days after the expected date, email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. I’ll send a new one or refund you.

Returns

Each print is made for you after you order, so I can’t accept returns or exchanges for a change of mind. Small color differences between screens and paper aren’t defects.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with. Most banks show them within 5 to 10 business days.

Questions?Email me at hello@tarverart.com or DM me on Instagram at @tarver.art. I answer every message myself, within 48 hours.
